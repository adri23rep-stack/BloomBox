BloomBox owner and whose source file **expressly refers to this document**,
the copyright holder adds the following requirement under AGPLv3 section 7(b):

> When conveying the covered original material, whether unchanged or in a
> modified work, preserve this specified reasonable author-attribution notice
> either in the corresponding source material or in the Appropriate Legal
> Notices displayed by a work containing the material:
>
> **Original BloomBox contributions (c) 2026 adri23rep-stack**
>

Equivalent formatting is permitted if the credit and source address are clear.
Keep the existing AGPL requirements to mark modified versions and their dates.
This provision does not require a splash screen, permanent logo, advertising,
credit for merely running the app, or credit for independent program output.
